Showrunner

Showrunner is a private desktop app that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps one creator make videos for their own channels and publish them to their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

How it uses TikTok

  • The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) once per channel, from the app's settings.
  • When a video is finished, the app shows the creator the post: the account it will go to, the caption, and TikTok's choices (who can view it, comments, Duet, Stitch, content disclosure). Nothing is chosen for them.
  • Only after the creator approves that exact post does the app upload the video to their own TikTok account (Content Posting API, Direct Post).
  • The app never posts to anyone else's account, never reads other users' data, and never posts without the creator's approval.

How it uses YouTube

  • The creator connects each channel once with "Sign in with Google", from the app's settings. The app asks only to upload videos and to read that channel's own name, handle and picture.
  • When a video is finished, the app shows the creator its title, description, tags, privacy setting and publish time. Only after the creator approves it does the app upload the video to their own channel (YouTube Data API).

How it uses Instagram

  • The creator connects their own professional Instagram account once, from the app's settings.
  • Only after the creator approves a post does the app publish that Reel or Story, with its caption, to their own account (Instagram API).

On every platform the app never posts to anyone else's account, never reads other users' data, and never posts without the creator's approval. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what data it uses.

Showrunner is not offered to the public; it is used only by its owner for their own channels.
