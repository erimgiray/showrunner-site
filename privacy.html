Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Showrunner ("the app") is a private desktop app that runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner for their own channels. This policy explains what it does with data from the platforms it connects to: YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What the app receives from YouTube (Google)

When the owner connects a YouTube channel with "Sign in with Google", the app asks for two permissions: youtube.upload (to upload videos) and youtube.readonly (to read the connected channel's own details).

  • The connected channel's ID, name, handle and profile picture, used only to show which channel is connected and to make sure a video goes to the right channel.
  • Access and refresh tokens, used only to upload the owner's approved videos to that same channel.

The app does not read the channel's videos, comments, subscribers, analytics or any other YouTube or Google account data, and it never reads data about other YouTube users.

Showrunner uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Showrunner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the signed-in account (open ID, display name, username, avatar), used only to show which account is connected and to make sure a video goes to the right channel.
  • Access and refresh tokens, used only to publish the owner's approved videos to that same account.
  • The creator settings TikTok returns before a post (such as the privacy options available), shown to the owner when they approve the post.

What the app receives from Instagram (Meta)

  • The connected professional account's ID, username, name and profile picture, used only to show which account is connected and to make sure a post goes to the right account.
  • An access token, used only to publish the owner's approved Reels and Stories to that same account.

Where it is kept

All of this is stored only on the owner's computer, in the app's local files. There is no Showrunner server or database. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

What the app sends

The app sends a video (or image) and its title, description or caption to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram only when the owner approves that post, and only to the owner's own connected account. It does not read, store or post anything for other users of those platforms.

Removing data

Disconnecting an account in the app's settings deletes its stored tokens. You can also remove the app's access at any time:

  • YouTube / Google: Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, or remove the app in Meta Business Settings.
