Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Showrunner ("the app") is a private desktop app that runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner for their own channels. This policy explains what it does with data from the platforms it connects to.

What the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the signed-in account (open ID, display name, username, avatar), used only to show which account is connected and to make sure a video goes to the right channel.
  • Access and refresh tokens, used only to publish the owner's approved videos to that same account.
  • The creator settings TikTok returns before a post (such as the privacy options available), shown to the owner when they approve the post.

Where it is kept

All of this is stored only on the owner's computer, in the app's local files. There is no Showrunner server or database. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

What the app sends

The app sends a video and its caption to TikTok only when the owner approves that post, and only to the owner's own connected account. It does not read, store or post anything for other TikTok users.

Removing data

Disconnecting an account in the app's settings deletes its stored tokens. You can also remove the app's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
